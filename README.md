One line a smart fridge would text its owner at 3am:

> 3am: your leftovers have unionized and demand a warmer shelf and fewer midnight visits.

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (87 characters, one line, UTF-8). Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-deysumbrella-the-3am-fridge-text-4c3e721d).
